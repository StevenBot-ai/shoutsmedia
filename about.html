About the Network

The Media Company Behind Texas Shoutouts™

Shoutouts Media™ is an independent media company built on a simple belief: every Texas community has stories worth telling. We launched the Texas Shoutouts™ Network to give local businesses, coaches, creators, and community heroes the spotlight they deserve - without the gatekeeping of big-market media.

Each publication in our network is locally focused, editorially independent, and powered by real community submissions. We don't manufacture stories. We amplify the ones already happening in your neighborhood - the 30-year family restaurant, the volunteer who shows up every week, the teacher who changes lives without recognition.

Shoutouts Media™ operates the Texas Shoutouts™ Network across 70+ communities statewide, with more markets launching throughout 2026. Our mission is to build the most trusted hyperlocal media network in Texas - and eventually, well beyond it.
